## 2. Business Overview

### Brand name

**ELORA**  
Persian display name: **الورا**

### Business descriptor

**مرکز تخصصی پوست، مو، زیبایی و جوان‌سازی الورا**

### Business concept

ELORA is intended to be a premium, science-led, patient-centered center for skin health, hair health, natural beauty, rejuvenation, and carefully selected regenerative or biologically based treatments. It must not be perceived as a conventional injection clinic or a volume-driven beauty salon.

The first center is planned for the Pasdaran / Dolat area of Tehran and is intended to become the first branch of a scalable chain. The brand must therefore work for the first location while remaining credible and transferable to future branches.

### Core business goal

Build one of Iran's most trusted premium brands in skin, hair, and responsible rejuvenation by combining medical assessment, evidence-aware treatment selection, personalization, natural-looking outcomes, privacy, and structured follow-up.

### Commercial objectives of the website

1. Convert suitable visitors into qualified consultation and appointment requests.
2. Establish credibility before the visitor contacts the center.
3. Explain why diagnosis must precede the sale of a service.
4. Differentiate ELORA from clinics built around discounts, trends, or exaggerated promises.
5. Educate visitors about skin, hair, aging, and advanced treatments in understandable language.
6. Support long-term care, repeat visits, and outcome follow-up.
7. Build a content and brand foundation that can scale to future ELORA branches.

### Non-goals

The website must not:

- promise guaranteed results;
- position every visitor as a candidate for every treatment;
- use fear, insecurity, or age-shaming to create demand;
- present experimental, unlicensed, or indication-inappropriate treatment as established routine care;
- reduce the brand to injections, devices, discounts, or before-and-after photographs;
- imply that beauty requires changing a person's identity or facial character.

## 3. What Has Been Learned About the Desired Project

The owner wants ELORA to feel premium, modern, calm, intelligent, feminine, and precise. Luxury should come from confidence, restraint, service quality, scientific credibility, and attention to detail—not from visual excess or extravagant claims.

The website must communicate that ELORA:

- understands the cause of a concern before recommending a procedure;
- treats each person as an individual rather than selling the same package to everyone;
- values natural beauty and identity-preserving outcomes;
- explains benefits, limitations, costs, possible adverse effects, and realistic expectations;
- accompanies the client from assessment through treatment, follow-up, and maintenance;
- has the discipline to decline an unsuitable treatment;
- uses innovation only when it has acceptable evidence, safety, legal status, and relevance;
- respects privacy, especially for consultations, payments, photography, and medical data;
- speaks effectively to teenagers and young women without sounding childish;
- speaks to women over 45 without using negative or ageist language;
- remains welcoming to men, while women are the primary audience;
- can become a trusted educational reference in Iran, not merely an advertising channel.

The digital experience and the physical clinic must tell the same story: quiet confidence, privacy, clarity, personalized care, and restrained luxury.

## 4. Brand Strategy

### Brand essence

**علم، اعتماد و زیبایی طبیعی**

### Primary brand statement

**زیبایی مبتنی بر علم؛ مراقبت مبتنی بر اعتماد**

### Supporting statement

**از تشخیص تخصصی تا درمان شخصی‌سازی‌شده و پیگیری نتیجه، در کنار شما هستیم تا سلامت پوست و مو و زیبایی طبیعی خود را با اطمینان بیشتری بازیابید.**

### Optional English brand line

**Beauty, Rooted in Science**

Use the English line only as a secondary brand expression. The Persian statement is the primary communication for the Iranian market.

### Brand promise

**پیش از پیشنهاد هر خدمت، مسئله شما را می‌شناسیم؛ پیش از آغاز درمان، مسیر را شفاف می‌کنیم؛ و پس از انجام خدمت، نتیجه را پیگیری می‌کنیم.**

### Positioning statement

For people who want healthier skin and hair and natural-looking aesthetic improvement, ELORA is a premium specialist center that combines medical assessment, evidence-aware care, personalization, privacy, and continuous follow-up. Unlike service-selling beauty clinics, ELORA begins with understanding the concern and recommends only treatments that are appropriate, explainable, and responsibly deliverable.

### Desired market position

ELORA should sit above an ordinary beauty clinic in scientific credibility, service experience, follow-up, and brand discipline. It should not claim absolute authority, guaranteed results, or regional leadership before these claims can be independently supported.

### Brand personality

- Calm, never loud
- Sophisticated, never showy
- Scientific, never cold or incomprehensible
- Caring, never sentimental or intrusive
- Feminine, never childish or exclusionary
- Confident, never absolute
- Innovative, never trend-chasing
- Premium, never sales-driven

### Tone of voice

ELORA speaks in clear, respectful, reassuring Persian. The tone should feel like a trusted physician and an attentive care guide speaking together.

Use language such as:

- ارزیابی تخصصی
- برنامه شخصی‌سازی‌شده
- نتیجه طبیعی
- انتخاب آگاهانه
- شواهد علمی
- ایمنی و تناسب درمان
- پیگیری نتیجه
- حفظ هویت طبیعی چهره
- ممکن است برای همه مناسب نباشد

Avoid language such as:

- درمان قطعی
- تضمین صددرصد
- معجزه
- بدون هیچ عارضه‌ای
- بهترین کلینیک ایران
- جوانی همیشگی
- نتیجه فوری و دائمی
- جدیدترین روش دنیا، without evidence or context
- expressions that shame age, acne, hair loss, or appearance

## 5. Brand Charter

1. سلامت مراجعه‌کننده بر فروش خدمات مقدم است.
2. هیچ نتیجه‌ای قطعی یا تضمینی معرفی نمی‌شود.
3. مزایا، محدودیت‌ها، عوارض احتمالی و هزینه‌ها شفاف توضیح داده می‌شوند.
4. فقط خدمات دارای توجیه پزشکی، ایمنی قابل‌قبول و پشتوانه علمی متناسب ارائه می‌شوند.
5. زیبایی طبیعی و هماهنگ با هویت فرد بر تغییرات افراطی اولویت دارد.
6. حریم خصوصی، تصاویر و اطلاعات پزشکی مراجعان محافظت می‌شوند.
7. مسئولیت ELORA با پایان جلسه درمان تمام نمی‌شود؛ نتیجه و مراقبت پس از درمان پیگیری می‌شود.
8. اگر درمانی برای فرد مناسب نباشد، حتی به قیمت از دست رفتن درآمد، انجام نمی‌شود.
9. دانش، مهارت و استانداردهای تیم به‌طور مستمر ارتقا می‌یابد.
10. اعتماد مراجعه‌کننده مهم‌ترین دارایی ELORA است.

## 6. Color Identity

The color identity is fixed in direction, although production values may be calibrated later for print and digital consistency.

| Color role | Recommended value | Meaning and use |
|---|---:|---|
| ELORA White | `#FBFAF8` | Primary brand field; purity, calm, clinical confidence |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean neutral support |
| Powder Pink | `#F3DFE3` | Soft, mature femininity and warmth; secondary color |
| Pale Blush | `#F8ECEE` | Quiet supportive background tone |
| Champagne Gold | `#C5A36A` | Premium accent; use sparingly, never as the dominant color |
| Warm Stone | `#D9D3CE` | Neutral support and visual balance |
| Warm Charcoal | `#332F30` | Primary readable text color; not a dark visual theme |
| Muted Taupe Gray | `#746D6B` | Secondary text and subdued information |

### Color rules

- White is dominant.
- Powder pink is very pale, refined, and secondary.
- Champagne gold is limited and minimal.
- Brown, coffee, Nescafé, burgundy, harsh black fields, and heavy dark sections are outside the approved identity.
- Pink must not become bright, sugary, childish, or cosmetic-retail pink.
- Gold must not become yellow, glossy, ornate, or visually dominant.
- The overall impression must remain modern European minimalism suitable for a premium medical environment.

## 7. Target Audiences

### Primary market

Affluent and upper-middle-income residents of Tehran, especially Pasdaran, Dolat, and nearby northern districts. They are detail-conscious, appearance-aware, digitally informed, and willing to pay for privacy, expertise, safety, personalization, and service quality.

### Audience segment 1: Teenage girls and young women

**Typical concerns:** acne, acne marks, pigmentation, oily skin, scalp dandruff, early hair shedding, and social self-consciousness.  
**Emotional need:** to be taken seriously without judgment, exaggeration, or unnecessary intervention.  
**Message:**

> ابتدا علت را پیدا می‌کنیم؛ سپس متناسب با سن، شرایط پوست یا مو و نیاز واقعی شما، مسیر مراقبت را انتخاب می‌کنیم.

**Important communication rule:** Never exploit insecurity or imply that normal adolescent appearance is defective. Where appropriate, encourage parent or guardian participation and medical referral.

### Audience segment 2: Women aged approximately 25–40

**Typical concerns:** skin clarity, uneven tone, pigmentation, acne history, hair quality, early signs of aging, preventive care, and natural aesthetic enhancement.  
**Emotional need:** polished results without an artificial or over-treated appearance.  
**Message:**

> زیبایی طبیعی با برنامه‌ای شخصی‌سازی‌شده؛ بدون تغییر اغراق‌آمیز در هویت چهره.

### Audience segment 3: Women and men aged 45+

**Typical concerns:** laxity, lines, volume changes, pigmentation, texture, hair thinning, and maintaining a healthy and rested appearance.  
**Emotional need:** to look healthier and refreshed without being told they must look younger or become someone else.  
**Message:**

> جوان‌سازی هوشمند با هدف بهبود کیفیت پوست و حفظ هویت طبیعی چهره.

### Audience segment 4: Men

**Typical concerns:** hair loss, scalp health, pigmentation, skin quality, and subtle rejuvenation.  
**Emotional need:** privacy, efficiency, medical clarity, and results that do not appear performative.  
**Message:**

> ارزیابی علمی، حفظ حریم خصوصی و برنامه‌ای متناسب با سبک زندگی شما.

### Audience segment 5: People interested in advanced regenerative treatments

**Typical concerns:** hair restoration, skin quality, healing, and interest in PRP, PRF, exosome-related products, or cell-based approaches.  
**Emotional need:** separating science from hype and understanding candidacy, evidence, legal status, and limitations.  
**Message:**

> روش‌های پیشرفته تنها پس از ارزیابی پزشکی و بررسی شواهد، ایمنی، مجوز و تناسب آن‌ها با شرایط شما پیشنهاد می‌شوند.

## 8. User Experience Principles

### Desired emotional journey

The visitor should move through these feelings:

1. **Recognition:** “They understand my concern.”
2. **Safety:** “They will not sell me a treatment before assessing me.”
3. **Clarity:** “I understand the process, limitations, and next step.”
4. **Confidence:** “The center appears medically responsible and attentive to detail.”
5. **Agency:** “I can make an informed decision without pressure.”
6. **Continuity:** “They will remain responsible for follow-up after the procedure.”

### Primary user tasks

- Identify the relevant concern: skin, hair, aging, or advanced treatment.
- Understand ELORA's approach before comparing individual procedures.
- Learn whether a consultation is necessary and what it includes.
- Review realistic information about a service without promotional exaggeration.
- Request a consultation or appointment.
- Access pre-treatment and post-treatment guidance after booking.
- Find address, contact information, hours, and privacy information.

### Experience rules

- Organize content around visitor concerns as well as treatment names.
- Explain medical terms in plain Persian when they first appear.
- Present consultation as a valuable clinical step, not an obstacle to booking.
- Keep the primary next step consistent: **درخواست ارزیابی و مشاوره**.
- Offer an alternate contact route for visitors who are not ready to book.
- Do not force a visitor to choose a procedure before assessment.
- State uncertainty honestly and explain that individual results vary.
- Give privacy special emphasis in photography, messaging, medical records, and payment-related interactions.
- Content for teenagers must be understandable and non-judgmental.
- Content for visitors aged 45+ must be respectful and free of age-shaming.
- The experience must remain welcoming to men without weakening the primarily female brand focus.

### Standard care journey

1. شناخت نیاز
2. ارزیابی تخصصی
3. طراحی برنامه شخصی
4. رضایت آگاهانه و درمان
5. پیگیری و سنجش نتیجه
6. مراقبت بلندمدت

Approved explanatory copy:

> در الورا، درمان با انتخاب یک دستگاه یا تزریق آغاز نمی‌شود. ابتدا نیاز، سابقه، شرایط پزشکی و انتظار شما بررسی می‌شود. سپس مسیر مناسب، مزایا، محدودیت‌ها، هزینه و مراقبت‌های لازم توضیح داده می‌شود. پس از درمان نیز نتیجه ثبت و پیگیری خواهد شد تا تصمیم‌های بعدی بر اساس پاسخ واقعی بدن شما گرفته شوند.
