## Service and page reuse

- Dedicated new portraits for pigmentation and scalp health remove the misleading reuse of acne-scar and hair-loss imagery.
- Hair loss, acne scars, microneedling, natural rejuvenation, Botox and the 45+ guide inherit the new matching article covers. This is reuse within the same subject, not a generic face applied to unrelated concerns.
- Skin, hair and rejuvenation category cards/pages now use the appropriate refreshed portraits. The advanced category keeps its conceptual scaffold.
- The four featured service portraits (PRP, mesogel, cell-based and regenerative) remain unchanged.
- The homepage regenerative-medicine explainer now uses the new research-workspace image. Its care-options journey image follows the updated personal follow-up article.
- About/contact/invitation retain the existing ELVITHA concept interiors. The homepage entrance and supplied portrait film remain appropriate. Care, FAQ, privacy and sitemap content do not need additional decorative portraits.

## Art direction and provenance

Adult portraits use silk scarf headbands, natural texture and modest clothing. The 45+ cover deliberately depicts a middle-aged woman; teenage-care artwork remains a care-kit still life. The condition covers are conceptual illustrations, not diagnostic references or photographs of patients or treatment outcomes. Research imagery depicts context without fabricated data or product claims.

The following references informed the restrained visual briefs; no reference photographs were copied or used as image inputs:

- [Depigmented patches, ordinary surface texture.](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/vitiligo-symptoms)
- [Restrained dry raised scaly elbow plaque.](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/psoriasis/what/symptoms)
- [Small violet bumps at the inner wrist.](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/lichen-planus-symptoms)
- [Distinguish color changes and shallow depressions.](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/acne/derm-treat/scars)
