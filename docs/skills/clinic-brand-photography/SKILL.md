# Clinic Brand Photography

Create attractive professional clinic editorial photographs with believable people, gestures, materials, and lighting. Brand consistency comes from color relationships and photographic quality; it does not require repeated faces, beige rooms, headscarves, or poses.

## Ground each image

Read the actual page title, introduction, relevant copy, image consumer, and intended crop before prompting. Inspect current and adjacent images when available. Determine service portrait, editorial topic image, skincare still life, or conceptual interior. Preserve conceptual-image disclosures and describe visible content in Persian alt text without making treatment claims.

Read the supporting references as needed: [Color and casting](references/color-and-casting.md), [Scene diversity](references/scene-diversity.md), and [Generation and review](references/generation-and-review.md).

## Essential creative direction

Collection size, route counts, and the 23–32 service casting rule below are defaults of the approved 2026-10-13 refresh. For future work, use the new request and live inventory; do not impose this batch's size or casting on unrelated clinic photography.

- Starting color proportions for wardrobe, background, and props are **65% neutral / 25% supporting color / 8% complementary accent / 2% metallic detail**, excluding skin and hair. Expressive compositions may use **55/30/12/3**. These are art-direction guides, not pixel quotas; do not add gratuitous metal.
- Anchor the brand with cream, blush, stone, and restrained champagne. Rotate rose/mineral teal, terracotta/slate blue, olive/plum, and caramel/blue-gray pairings. Strong color may sit in a garment, wall, or textile; skin remains credible.
- Target roughly **40% warm / 35% cool / 25% balanced lighting** across the collection. For 42 photos, 17 warm / 15 cool / 10 balanced is useful rounding. Coordinate the shared ledger rather than imposing that ratio on every small batch.
- For the current approved 2026-10-13 refresh, every service portrait uses a clearly adult Persian woman approximately **23–32**, including the 45+ service. Age-specific articles, including teen and 45+ topics, use skincare or preparation objects instead of conflicting portrait ages. This is editorial casting, not a treatment recommendation.
- Cast distinct people: vary face shape, complexion, nose/brows, hair texture/length/color, makeup, garment construction, expression, and posture. Persian identity is not one mandatory facial template. Scarves may appear occasionally without becoming a uniform.
- Use a **balanced enhancement continuum**: mostly understated faces, with a smaller group showing moderately fuller lips or more defined contours. Describe lip fullness, cheek definition, and makeup independently. For 16 service portraits, about 11 understated and 5 moderate is a useful starting mix. Never claim a synthetic model actually received a procedure.
- Preserve pores, normal tonal variation, small asymmetries, flyaway hair, fabric texture, and plausible reflections. Carefully edited and well-groomed can still be natural.
- Product-related scenes use relevant **unbranded skincare objects** and honest materials. There is no commercial product catalog; do not invent labels, brands, efficacy, or partnerships.
- Avoid forced smiles, theatrical clinic poses, decorative medical equipment, fake results, plastic skin, excessive props, and repeating beige-room compositions.

## Brainstorm before generating

Write **three materially different concepts per image**, then choose one by topic fit, crop safety, and collection variety. Each concept specifies setting, action, framing, light, palette, and styling or object materials. Changing only garment color does not create a different concept.

Rotate purposeful skin/hair-care moments, consultation preparation, dressing areas, architectural shade, simple colored portrait sets, and relevant still lifes. Mix environmental portraits, closer details, three-quarter views, seated gestures, and standing poses. Neighboring images should differ across several of setting, pose, light, palette, framing, hair, and wardrobe.

Keep a job ledger with topic/placement, three concepts, selection reason, scene, light class, palette, framing, action, casting, hair, wardrobe, independent cosmetic-feature descriptions, final prompt, actual source/output paths, dimensions/hashes, focal position, Persian alt/caption, and review. Intentional reuse of one master across related placements is valid; unrelated masters remain distinct.

## Generate with built-in imagegen

Apply the available imagegen skill. Use the **built-in imagegen tool**, one independent photograph per call. A batch is separate coordinated photographs, never a generated contact sheet, grid, collage, or panels sliced into substitutes. Do not switch to API/CLI generation without explicit user authorization.

For local edits, inspect every reference with view_image first, label its role, then use the current supported reference mechanism. Keep exact invariants in the prompt. Obtain the actual generated output path and copy accepted originals into the project; never invent paths or leave a website asset only in the Codex output directory.

Begin a substantial collection with representative portrait, cosmetics, skincare, editorial, and architecture images. Review internally and refine demonstrated problems before completing the collection; do not add an unrequested approval gate.

## Review and integrate

Inspect full resolution and intended desktop, square mobile, and landscape-card crops. Check anatomy, hands, reflections, skin, hair, fabric, topic relevance, relaxed posture, and absence of unwanted text. Compare a contact sheet for repeated faces, gaze, hair silhouettes, rooms, props, and dominant colors. Correct specific visible defects and record factual acceptance observations.

Copy accepted originals into the project and create versioned full/800px WebP derivatives without upscaling. Preserve the current JournalImage interface, lazy loading, responsive sources, and accurate dimensions. Set shot-specific focal positions and check all actual crops. Target the corridor still at **120KB or less**. Keep old production files until every consumer is verified.
